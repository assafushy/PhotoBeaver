## M5 - Faces and people (2026-10-04)

| D114 | Face models                    | `enricher-faces` uses InsightFace `buffalo_l` (SCRFD-10G detection, ArcFace `w600k_r50`, 512-d). The models are licensed for non-commercial research only, so they are never committed: the plugin downloads `buffalo_l.zip` from InsightFace's GitHub release when turned on, verifies SHA-256 of the zip and both extracted models, and keeps only `det_10g.onnx` and `w600k_r50.onnx` in its data folder.                                                                                                                                   | Your choice: best accuracy and the spec's 512-d; the MIT repo stays clean.                                                                                                                                              | Approved in M5 plan |
| D115 | Face runtime                   | Native `onnxruntime-node` 1.30 in the plugin host (`nativeModules: true`). Verified in an Electron 44 utilityProcess and in the packaged app.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Your choice; fast, multi-threaded.                                                                                                                                                                                      | Approved in M5 plan |
| D116 | Clustering in core             | The plugin only detects faces and computes embeddings; a core `PeopleService` groups faces and owns names, merges and splits (spec 6.3/9.3 put clustering in the plugin's `finalize`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Your choice: user edits are never overwritten, and another face plugin can reuse the People screen.                                                                                                                     | Approved in M5 plan |
| D117 | Clustering method              | Incremental, like Immich: a face joins the person of its nearest assigned neighbour within cosine distance 0.5 unless that pairing was rejected; a face with at least 2 unassigned neighbours within 0.5 starts a new unnamed person; the rest wait. Runs as a debounced durable `cluster_faces` job after face results, and once at startup. Distance and minimum faces per person come from the plugin's settings.                                                                                                                           | Works with sqlite-vec KNN and no extra dependencies; measured same-person distances 0.14 to 0.34 and different people 1.06 to 1.12 on the test portraits.                                                               | Accepted            |
| D118 | User edits win                 | Moving faces (split) sets `assigned_by = 'user'`, which clustering never touches. "Not this person" unassigns the face and records `face_rejections(face_id, person_id)`, so clustering never puts it back with that person but may assign it elsewhere.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Fixing clusters must stick.                                                                                                                                                                                             | Accepted            |
| D119 | Stable face ids                | When an enricher re-runs, detected faces are matched to existing ones by box overlap (IoU at least 0.5, user-assigned faces first); a match keeps its id, person and assignment. Unmatched old faces are deleted with their embeddings.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Re-run would otherwise lose names and fixes.                                                                                                                                                                            | Accepted            |
| D120 | Faces when merging copies      | If the survivor already has faces from the same plugin, the merged copy's faces are dropped instead of doubled, and their people pass to the overlapping survivor faces. The merge snapshot stores the merged faces with embeddings; undo restores them (with their person if it still exists). Person changes made to survivor faces are not reverted by undo.                                                                                                                                                                                | Two identical photos would otherwise count every person twice.                                                                                                                                                          | Accepted            |
| D121 | sqlite-vec                     | `faces_vec USING vec0(face_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, embedding float[512] distance_metric=cosine)` in a custom migration. The extension (sqlite-vec 0.1.9, a dependency of `@photobeaver/db` and the app) loads right after the pragmas on every open, before migrations; in a packaged app its path is rewritten to `app.asar.unpacked`. Vectors are deleted explicitly with faces, and maintenance prunes orphans after cascading deletes.                                                                                                        | Virtual tables have no foreign keys.                                                                                                                                                                                    | Approved in M5 plan |
| D122 | People cleanup                 | Maintenance deletes people left without faces and clears covers that point at deleted faces.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Purges and merges can empty a person.                                                                                                                                                                                   | Accepted            |
| D123 | Face crops                     | `pb-media://face/<faceId>` serves a 256 px square crop (1.6 times the face box, clamped to the image) cut from the 1024 thumbnail with sharp and cached under `thumbs/faces/`. Crops are not refreshed when a face box moves slightly on re-run.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Cheap and private; no extra files per face until viewed.                                                                                                                                                                | Accepted            |
| D124 | People in search               | Named people are part of `assets_fts`; every people change refreshes search text for the affected assets. The library filter has `personIds` and the facets list named people.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | SPEC 8.1 #3.                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Accepted            |
| D125 | Person page                    | The person page shows the faces for rename, merge and split, and a "Show photos" link that opens the library filtered to that person, instead of an embedded grid.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Reuses the grid and viewer with their keyboard handling.                                                                                                                                                                | Accepted            |
| D126 | Enable notice                  | Additive manifest field `enableNotice { title, body, url? }`; turning on such a plugin shows it in a consent dialog. Its link opens only through `plugins.openNotice`, which opens the https URL from that plugin's own manifest.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | The face models' license must be accepted before download; the renderer gets no arbitrary link opener.                                                                                                                  | Approved in M5 plan |
| D127 | Plugin activity                | Additive `ctx.status(text)` shows a short line on the plugin card (for example "Downloading face models: 45%"), carried as `activity` in the plugin summary.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Large downloads need visible progress.                                                                                                                                                                                  | Approved in M5 plan |
| D128 | Model download                 | Download starts in `activate` (or the first `enrich`), with progress per percent, through `.partial` files and a rename after the hash matches; failures retry with backoff from 30 s to 30 min. Until ready, `enrich` throws `RateLimitedError(30 s)` so jobs wait without failing. Verified hashes are cached by size and mtime.                                                                                                                                                                                                             | No re-hashing 190 MB on every start.                                                                                                                                                                                    | Accepted            |
| D129 | Native dependencies in plugins | A plugin's package.json may declare `photobeaver.nativeDependencies`; `pb-plugin build` keeps them external and copies them with their production dependencies (flat, first version wins) into `dist/node_modules`, pruning other platforms' binaries (and onnxruntime's duplicate versioned dylib on macOS). `dev` copies them once per run. validate and pack require `permissions.nativeModules: true`; pack refuses more than 512 MB. Installing a plugin keeps `dist/node_modules` and skips only the plugin folder's own `node_modules`. | onnxruntime-node can't be bundled; each OS build ships its own binaries (46 MB on macOS arm64).                                                                                                                         | Approved in M5 plan |
| D130 | onnxruntime install script     | `onnxruntime-node: false` under `allowBuilds` in pnpm-workspace.yaml: its install script only fetches optional CUDA parts, and the prebuilt binaries ship in the package.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | pnpm 12 refuses to install with unapproved build scripts.                                                                                                                                                               | Accepted            |
| D131 | Face plugin details            | Detection letterboxes to 640, decodes SCRFD anchors at strides 8/16/32 with keypoints, NMS 0.4; alignment is a least-squares similarity onto the ArcFace template with bilinear sampling; embeddings are L2-normalized. At most 50 faces per image. Settings: minimum face size (shorter side, thumbnail pixels), detection threshold, cluster distance, minimum faces per person, each clamped to a range.                                                                                                                                    | Port of InsightFace's reference pipeline in TypeScript.                                                                                                                                                                 | Accepted            |
| D132 | Real-model test                | A gated test runs the real models on public-domain NASA and US Air Force portraits committed under `plugins/enricher-faces/tests/fixtures` (attribution in ATTRIBUTION.md). CI runs it on Ubuntu with the models downloaded once (checksum verified) and cached; elsewhere it is skipped.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Keeps the model pipeline honest without downloading 290 MB on every OS.                                                                                                                                                 | Accepted            |
| D133 | Stopping a starting host       | `HostHandle.stop()` waits up to 3 s for a host that is still starting, then closes its connection and kills it; a start that began before a stop gives up.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Windows CI logs showed core shutdown finishing in about 1 s while the app still did not exit: a plugin host launched during startup was left running when quit came right after launch.                                 | Accepted            |
| D134 | Bounded close in e2e           | E2E tests close the app through `closeApp`: if `close()` takes over 20 s, it records a `quit-hang` annotation with whether the main process is alive and the Electron process list (Windows), then kills the process tree. Quit events and shutdown steps are logged and flushed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | A Windows-only hang when quitting right after launch kept failing one test after two root-cause fixes; this keeps CI meaningful while collecting the evidence to find the cause. Open: the root cause is not yet known. | Replaced by D150    |

On Linux the `basic_text` backend is refused with a message to install a keyring. A cancelled or failed add deletes any partial secret; removing a source deletes its secret.                                                                                                                                                                         | SPEC 10: tokens only through safeStorage; core stays headless.                                                                                                                   | Approved in M4 plan |

| D100 | Setup cancel and status             | `sources.add` and `sources.reconnect` take a `setupId`; `sources.cancelSetup` aborts it, and the UI shows "Finish signing in in your browser" for connectors with OAuth permission. | D101 | `ctx.ui.openExternal`               | Additive: opens an https URL in the system browser only when its host is in the plugin's network allowlist. e2e tests of unpackaged builds set `PB_E2E_NO_BROWSER` so nothing opens.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | The Picker must open its `pickerUri`.                                                                                                                                            | Approved in M4 plan |
| D102 | Redirects and the allowlist         | No extra redirect check in `ctx.fetch`: Node's fetch opens sockets through the patched `net`/`tls`, so a redirect to a host outside the allowlist is already refused (verified).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Avoids reimplementing redirect handling.                                                                                                                                         | Accepted            |
| D103 | Per-source plugin data              | Plugins keep source-specific files under `<dataDir>/sources/<sourceId>`; core deletes that folder when the source is removed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Picker previews would otherwise be left behind.                                                                                                                                  | Accepted            |
| D104 | SDK HTTP helpers                    | `@photobeaver/plugin-sdk/http`: `apiFetch`/`fetchJson` (a 401 refreshes once then `AuthRequiredError`, a final 429 becomes `RateLimitedError` with Retry-After, else `HttpError`) and `tokenStore` (refresh 60 s before expiry, keep the old refresh token when none is returned, network errors rethrown so the sync retries).                                                                                                                                                                           | Shared by all cloud connectors; default plugins import only the SDK.                                                                                                             | Accepted            |
| D105 | Contract suite fixtures             | `ConnectorFixture.context` passes fake host options (fetch, seeded secret, settings, OAuth) to every check. Thumbnails use the file id.                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Dropbox listings have no ids on deleted entries.                                                                                                                                 | Accepted            |
| D107 | OneDrive mapping                    | Graph delta with nextLink and deltaLink as cursors; 410 restarts a full scan. Paths come from a folder map in plugin storage (delta omits `parentReference.path`). `contentHash { algo: 'quickxor' }`, or sha256 when that is all Graph gives. Originals and thumbnails are fetched from pre-authenticated URLs without the Authorization header. | D108 | Takeout mapping                     | A Google Photos folder is found by its media having sidecars (works for localized names). Sidecars: `.supplemental-metadata.json`, older `.json`, names clipped at 46 characters, `(n)` copies and `-edited` copies. `geoData` before `geoDataExif`; 0,0 means none.                                                                                                                             | Takeout's real naming rules.                                                                                                                                                     | Accepted            |

| D110 | Archive cursors                     | Archive connectors emit `after:<id>`-style cursors mid-scan and a `done` cursor with `isFullScan: true` on the last batch, so the next sync starts a fresh full scan; etag is `${size}-${modifiedAt}` of the entry.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | A last-id cursor would make the next sync see nothing and tombstone everything.                                                                                                  | Accepted            |
| D111 | Archive and Picker sync modes       | Picker, Takeout, Facebook and Instagram sources are `manual` (`defaultIntervalSec` 86400 because the schema requires it).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Exports don't change on their own; the Picker needs the user.                                                                                                                    | Accepted            |
| D112 | CI test concurrency                 | CI runs package tests two at a time (`turbo run test --concurrency=2`); local `pnpm test` stays fully parallel.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | With 13 packages in parallel on 3 to 4 core runners, hashing and file-heavy tests missed their timeouts at random.                                                               | Accepted            |
| D113 | Quit never hangs                    | Quitting gives core shutdown 10 s, then exits; after a clean shutdown, `app.exit` is a 5 s backstop if Electron's own quit stalls. Shutdown steps are logged.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Windows CI showed the app hanging on close right after a first-run launch with nine default plugins. Jobs are durable and the database uses WAL, so a forced exit loses nothing. | Accepted            |

### Follow-ups from the M0 review (2026-10-03)

- **D14:** the grid orders by `COALESCE(captured_at, 0)` but the index is on plain `captured_at`, so SQLite cannot use it and sorts the whole table on every page. Add a matching expression index.
